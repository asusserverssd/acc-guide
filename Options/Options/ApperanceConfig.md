# Appearance Configuration

The **Appearance Configuration** allows you to customize and personalize your ASUS Control Center’s top left banner logo.

To access **Appearance Configuration**, click ![Options icon](../../IMG/icon-options_02.png) in the left menu, then click on **Appearance Configuration**.

![Appearance Configuration page showing the current banner logo with the Upload and Reset buttons](../../IMG/8-3_p07-1_appearance-config-page.png)

## Setting a custom banner logo

1. Click on **Upload**, then select your new banner logo.

    > **NOTE:** The height dimension of the logo image file should be 56 pixels.

    ![Upload button highlighted, with a file picker open to select the new banner logo](../../IMG/8-3_p08-1_banner-upload.png)

2. Once you have finished uploading the new banner logo, click on **Save**.

    ![Save button highlighted after the new banner logo is uploaded](../../IMG/8-3_p08-2_banner-save.png)

    ![New banner logo highlighted at the top left of the banner](../../IMG/8-3_p08-3_banner-result.png)

## Resetting the banner logo

1. Click on **Reset** to reset your banner logo to the default banner logo.

    ![Reset button highlighted next to the banner logo field](../../IMG/8-3_p09-1_banner-reset.png)

2. Click on **Save** to save the changes made.

    ![Save button highlighted after the banner logo is reset to the default](../../IMG/8-3_p09-2_banner-reset-save.png)
